# Privacy Policy — SweetTeaSupply Pin Manager

Effective date: October 19, 2026

SweetTeaSupply Pin Manager ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the SweetTeaSupply Etsy shop to manage that shop's own Pinterest business account.

## What the app accesses
- The SweetTeaSupply Pinterest business account only: its boards, pins, and pin statistics.
- Public information about SweetTeaSupply's own Etsy listings (titles, images, links) used to create pins.

## What the app does with it
- Creates pins and boards on the SweetTeaSupply Pinterest account that link to SweetTeaSupply's Etsy listings.
- Reads pin statistics (such as clicks) to understand which products get interest.

## What the app does not do
- It does not access, collect, store, or sell any other Pinterest user's personal information.
- It is not offered to the public, and no other people can log in to or use it.
- It does not share data with third parties or use it for advertising.

## Storage and security
- Access keys and statistics are stored on the owner's private, encrypted drive and are never published or shared.
- Data is kept only as long as needed to run the shop and can be deleted by the owner at any time.
